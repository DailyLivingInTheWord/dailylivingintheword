Why Theology Matters

Theology is not merely academic.

What we believe about God shapes how we worship Him, how we understand salvation, how we respond to suffering, and how we live the Christian life.

Theology begins with Scripture. It asks us to slow down, read carefully, understand what God has revealed, and consider the implications for our lives.

The goal is not simply to know more theological terms. It is to know God rightly and to live faithfully before Him.
